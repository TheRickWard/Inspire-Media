Load the music for your event

  1. In the portal, open your competition's Music tab and press Download all music (whole event or one session).
  2. Here, press Load music and choose that zip file. You can also choose music files straight from a folder or USB stick.
  3. The songs appear in running order and are kept on this device, so the player works with no internet.

Install this page as an app so it opens offline: on Windows use Edge or Chrome's Install app button in the address bar; on iPad tap Share then Add to Home Screen, and load the music inside the installed app.
